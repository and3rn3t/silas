# Silas's Personal Website - User Guide 🎮

Welcome to your personal website, Silas! This guide will help you understand how to use all the features.

## 🌟 Getting Started

To open your website, simply open the `index.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.).

## 📱 Navigation

Your website has 5 main sections:
- **Home** - Your bio and interests
- **Gallery** - Your picture collection
- **Stories** - Your written stories
- **Adventure Game** - Play the mini RPG game
- **Edit** - Add and manage your content

Click on any section in the navigation bar to switch between them.

## 🏠 Home Section

This is your main page where visitors can learn about you!

- **About Me**: A short bio about yourself
- **My Interests**: Tags showing what you love

You can edit these in the "Edit" section.

## 🖼️ Gallery Section

This is where all your pictures are displayed.

**To add pictures:**
1. Go to the "Edit" section
2. Find the "Add Picture" panel
3. Enter the image URL (link to the picture on the internet)
4. Write a caption for the picture
5. Click "Add Picture"

**To delete pictures:**
- Click the "×" button in the top-right corner of any picture
- Confirm that you want to delete it

## 📚 Stories Section

Share your creative stories here!

**To add stories:**
1. Go to the "Edit" section
2. Find the "Add Story" panel
3. Enter a title for your story
4. Write your story in the text box
5. Click "Add Story"

**To delete stories:**
- Click the "Delete" button on any story
- Confirm that you want to delete it

## 🗡️ Adventure Game

This is your mini RPG adventure game!

### Game Stats:
- **Hero**: Your character's name (currently "Brave Adventurer")
- **Level**: Your current level
- **XP**: Experience points (level up when you reach the needed amount)
- **HP**: Health points (don't let this reach 0!)
- **Gold**: Money you've collected

### Game Actions:

**🗺️ Explore**
- Go on adventures to different locations
- 50% chance to find treasure
- 50% chance to fight monsters
- Gain XP, gold, and level up!
- Be careful - monsters can damage you

**😴 Rest**
- Restore 50% of your health
- Use this when your HP is low
- No cost!

**🏪 Shop**
- Buy items with your gold
- Health Potion (50 gold) - Restore 50 HP
- Max HP Upgrade (100 gold) - Permanently increase max HP by 20

**🔄 New Game**
- Start over from level 1
- Warning: This deletes all your progress!

### Tips:
- Keep your HP high by resting regularly
- Save up gold to buy upgrades
- The higher your level, the more XP you need to level up
- Each level increases your max HP by 20

## ✏️ Edit Section

This is your control panel for managing all content!

### Edit Bio
1. Type your bio in the text box
2. Click "Save Bio"
3. Your bio will update on the Home page

### Edit Interests
- **Add Interest**: Type a new interest and click "Add Interest"
- **Remove Interest**: Click the "×" button next to any interest

### Add Picture
1. Enter the URL of the picture (must be a link from the internet)
2. Enter a caption/description
3. Click "Add Picture"

**Finding Picture URLs:**
- Right-click on any image online
- Select "Copy Image Address" or "Copy Image URL"
- Paste it into the Picture URL field

### Add Story
1. Enter a creative title
2. Write your story in the large text box
3. Click "Add Story"

## 💾 How Your Data is Saved

Your website uses something called "Local Storage" to save your data:
- Everything is saved in your web browser
- Your data persists even after closing the browser
- Each browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) stores data separately
- Clearing browser data will delete your saved content

**Important**: Always use the same browser to see your saved content!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Pictures not showing?**
- Make sure the URL is correct
- The image link must work (try opening it in a new tab first)
- Some websites don't allow their images to be displayed elsewhere

**Data disappeared?**
- Make sure you're using the same browser
- Check if browser data was cleared
- Try refreshing the page

**Game not working?**
- Try clicking "New Game" to reset
- Refresh the page
